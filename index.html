About Us

Clear Vision. Trusted Care.

At Shresht Eye Care, we provide comprehensive eye care with compassion and expertise. Our clinic offers advanced treatments ranging from sutureless cataract surgery to glaucoma management — ensuring your eyes get the best care possible.

What We Offer

Sutureless Cataract Surgery

Quick recovery and sharper vision using advanced phacoemulsification.

Glaucoma Evaluation & Treatment

Early detection and effective management to protect your eyesight.

Dry Eye Treatment

Relief for burning, irritation, and discomfort with tailored treatment.

Pterygium Excision Surgery

Safe surgical care for clear and healthy eyes.

Retina Evaluation

Advanced screening for diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration & more.

DCR / DCT Surgery
